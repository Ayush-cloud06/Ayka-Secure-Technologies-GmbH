Ayka Secure Technologies GmbH is a simulated company; this document does not establish a performed activity.

## 1. Purpose and scheduling

Prepare management review under ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause 9.3. Hold a review at least annually after ISMS adoption and after a material incident, scope change or risk escalation where decisions cannot wait. Managing Director chairs; CISO coordinates; [role owners](../../../organization/roles-and-responsibilities.md) supply evidence. This agenda is **Designed** and records no scheduled meeting, attendance, decision, approval or minutes. A tabletop walk-through exists ([SIM-MR-001](../../records/simulated/management-review/SIM-MR-001-management-review-2026.md), simulated); it is not a review.

Before the first review, appoint actual participants and agree date, period, evidence cutoff and authority. One maintainer holding several roles does not establish independent management scrutiny. Record absent functions and unresolved capacity explicitly.

## 2. Pre-read and required inputs

CISO requests the following at least five business days before the agreed meeting as a proposed preparation target. Each input owner delivers by that date; quarterly metric results are due as set in [ISMS-MON-01](../08-monitoring-and-measurement/kpis-and-metrics.md) section 1. Sources must state dates, period and gaps; if an activity has not happened, say so.

The rows follow clause 9.3.2 items a) to g) in order (working labels, not the standard's text), so a reviewer can tick each one off. Item d) has four parts.

| Clause 9.3.2 | Agenda item | Input / source | Presenter role / decision sought |
|---|---|---|---|
| a) | Status of actions from previous reviews | [Action log](management-review-action-log.md), overdue and effectiveness evidence. At the first review there is no previous review: review instead the management decisions taken outside a review (for example approvals of the ISMS documents) | CISO / close only verified actions; decide escalations |
| b) | Changes in external and internal issues | [Context](../00-context-and-governance/context-of-the-organization.md), scope, climate relevance, staffing, service, operations and dependency changes (incidents, restore tests, supplier changes, data location facts) | CISO / confirm scope and change needs |
| c) | Changes in needs and expectations of interested parties | [Interested parties](../00-context-and-governance/interested-parties.md), [legal register](../00-context-and-governance/legal-and-regulatory-register.md), new or changed contracts | Compliance & Risk Management / assign verification or response |
| d) 1 | Performance: nonconformities and corrective actions | [NC/CA log](../11-improvement/nonconformity-and-corrective-action-log.md), recurrence, overdue/ineffective actions | CISO / resolve blockers and accountability |
| d) 2 | Performance: monitoring and measurement results | [Metrics](../08-monitoring-and-measurement/kpis-and-metrics.md) with trends, access reviews, supplier reviews and measurement gaps | Operational owners / decide priorities and resources; do not infer quarterly performance from one run |
| d) 3 | Performance: audit results | Actual reports and [findings log](../09-internal-audit/audit-findings-log.md), programme coverage and independence limitations | CISO and appointed auditor / resource follow-up or first audit |
| d) 4 | Performance: fulfilment of objectives | [ISMS objectives](../00-context-and-governance/isms-objectives.md) and their measures | CISO / confirm, change or resource objectives |
| e) | Feedback from interested parties | Complaints, customer and supplier feedback, questionnaires received, regulator contact | Compliance & Risk Management / decide responses |
| f) | Risk assessment results and treatment status | [Risk register](../03-risk-management/risk-register.md), treatment status, residual decisions and SoA changes | CISO / accept, mitigate, avoid or transfer only with recorded rationale |
| g) | Opportunities for continual improvement | [Improvement register](../11-improvement/improvement-register.md), lessons and proposals | All function owners / select changes and funding |

If a function is absent, record it. A decision that assigns work, resources or risk to the absent function is recorded as provisional and confirmed with that function's owner within 10 business days, or brought back to the next review.

## 3. Decisions and outputs

For each actual decision record issue, evidence considered, options, rationale, authorized decision maker, owner role, due date/trigger, resources and verification method. Outputs must cover opportunities for continual improvement and any required ISMS changes; document resource decisions and explicit deferrals. Risk acceptance is a distinct authorized decision under the [risk methodology](../03-risk-management/risk-management-methodology.md), not implied by discussion or silence.

The initial review should explicitly decide document approval, ownership/resources, independent audit/review capacity, real-event evidence collection, identity/state/live-operation prerequisites and the Region mismatch. These are proposed agenda items, not predetermined decisions.

## 4. Record and follow-up

Create minutes only after the meeting. Include actual date/participants, review period, inputs/version references, significant discussion/objections, decisions and [action IDs](management-review-action-log.md). Circulate for factual confirmation and record approval when it occurs. CISO tracks actions monthly and reports material delays to Managing Director; link verified closure evidence. Store sensitive minutes under the [retention policy](../../policies/records-and-data-retention-policy.md) and publish only sanitized references. No management-review minutes are created by this agenda.
