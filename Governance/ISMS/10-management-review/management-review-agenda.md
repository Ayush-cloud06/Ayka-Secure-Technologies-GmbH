Ayka Secure Technologies GmbH is a simulated company; this document does not establish a performed activity.

## 1. Purpose and scheduling

Prepare management review under ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause 9.3. Hold a review at least annually after ISMS adoption and after a material incident, scope change or risk escalation where decisions cannot wait. Managing Director chairs; CISO coordinates; [role owners](../../../organization/roles-and-responsibilities.md) supply evidence. This agenda is **Designed** and records no scheduled meeting, attendance, decision, approval or minutes.

Before the first review, appoint actual participants and agree date, period, evidence cutoff and authority. One maintainer holding several roles does not establish independent management scrutiny. Record absent functions and unresolved capacity explicitly.

## 2. Pre-read and required inputs

CISO requests the following at least five business days before the agreed meeting as a proposed preparation target. Sources must state dates, period and gaps; if an activity has not happened, say so.

| Agenda item | Input / source | Presenter role / decision sought |
|---|---|---|
| Previous actions | [Action log](management-review-action-log.md), overdue and effectiveness evidence | CISO / close only verified actions; decide escalations |
| Changes in internal/external issues | [Context](../00-context-and-governance/context-of-the-organization.md), scope, climate relevance, staffing and service changes | CISO / confirm scope and change needs |
| Interested-party needs | [Interested parties](../00-context-and-governance/interested-parties.md), complaints, customer/supplier feedback, [legal register](../00-context-and-governance/legal-and-regulatory-register.md) | Compliance & Risk Management / assign verification or response |
| Security performance and objectives | [Metrics](../08-monitoring-and-measurement/kpis-and-metrics.md), objectives, trends and measurement gaps | Operational owners / decide priorities/resources; do not infer quarterly performance from one run |
| Nonconformities and corrective actions | [NC/CA log](../11-improvement/nonconformity-and-corrective-action-log.md), recurrence, overdue/ineffective actions | CISO / resolve blockers and accountability |
| Audit results | Actual reports and [findings log](../09-internal-audit/audit-findings-log.md), programme coverage and independence limitations | CISO and appointed auditor / resource follow-up or first audit |
| Risk and treatment | [Risk register](../03-risk-management/risk-register.md), treatment status, residual decisions and SoA changes | CISO / accept, mitigate, avoid or transfer only with recorded rationale |
| Operations and dependencies | Incidents/exercises, restore tests, access reviews, supplier reviews and data/Region facts | Security Operations and Cloud Platform Engineering / resource reliability and control gaps |
| Continual improvement | [Improvement register](../11-improvement/improvement-register.md), lessons and proposals | All function owners / select changes and funding |

## 3. Decisions and outputs

For each actual decision record issue, evidence considered, options, rationale, authorized decision maker, owner role, due date/trigger, resources and verification method. Outputs must cover opportunities for continual improvement and any required ISMS changes; document resource decisions and explicit deferrals. Risk acceptance is a distinct authorized decision under the [risk methodology](../03-risk-management/risk-management-methodology.md), not implied by discussion or silence.

The initial review should explicitly decide document approval, ownership/resources, independent audit/review capacity, real-event evidence collection, identity/state/live-operation prerequisites and the Region mismatch. These are proposed agenda items, not predetermined decisions.

## 4. Record and follow-up

Create minutes only after the meeting. Include actual date/participants, review period, inputs/version references, significant discussion/objections, decisions and [action IDs](management-review-action-log.md). Circulate for factual confirmation and record approval when it occurs. CISO tracks actions monthly and reports material delays to Managing Director; link verified closure evidence. Store sensitive minutes under the [retention policy](../../policies/records-and-data-retention-policy.md) and publish only sanitized references. No management-review minutes are created by this agenda.
