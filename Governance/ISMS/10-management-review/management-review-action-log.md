## 1. Purpose and boundary

Track actions actually assigned through [management review](management-review-agenda.md). This is a record schema, not evidence that review occurred. Proposed agenda items and risk treatments do not become management decisions until an authorized meeting or separately recorded management decision has happened.

## 2. Entry and progress rules

CISO assigns `MR-<year>-<sequence>` after a real decision and links the approved minutes/decision, source input and owner role. Each action includes the outcome sought, due date or explicit prerequisite trigger, resource decision and measurable acceptance/effectiveness criteria. Use a real calendar date when management schedules work; do not invent dates or signatures to fill the table.

Statuses: Open, In progress, Blocked, Awaiting verification, Closed. A blocked action retains its owner, reason and next escalation date. Changes to scope/due date require the decision maker's recorded approval; preserve the original commitment and reason. Closure requires the evidence and verifier specified by the decision. High-risk or self-owned action closure needs another competent reviewer; one person changing roles is not independent review.

## 4. Review and retention

CISO reviews progress monthly once actions exist and prepares the prior-action input for the next management review. Escalate missed dates and material blockers to Managing Director; reopen ineffective actions. An empty log does not demonstrate that all actions are complete. Retain decision history and restricted evidence under the [retention policy](../../policies/records-and-data-retention-policy.md). ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clauses 9.3 and 10.1.
