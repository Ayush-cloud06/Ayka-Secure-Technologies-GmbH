## 3. Proposed substantive terms

1. **Protected information.** Non-public customer evidence, personal information, source/design material, credentials, business records and other information reasonably understood or identified as confidential. Public information is excluded unless it became public through a breach. Exclusions also cover information demonstrably lawfully known, independently developed or lawfully obtained without a confidentiality duty.
2. **Permitted use.** Use protected information only for the recorded purpose. Access and disclose only to authorised recipients who need it and are bound by appropriate confidentiality duties. No publication, unauthorised copy, personal-cloud transfer or use for another customer or business.
3. **Safeguards.** Follow the disclosed classification, access, encryption and incident-reporting requirements. Do not share credentials. Use approved equipment and transfer channels; report suspected loss or disclosure promptly to the recorded contact.
4. **Required disclosures and protected reports.** Nothing prevents a disclosure required by law, lawful cooperation with authorities, obtaining confidential legal advice or protected reporting. Where lawful, notify the disclosing party promptly of compelled disclosure and disclose only what is required. Review whistleblowing and employment-law safeguards for the actual agreement.
5. **Return and deletion.** At request or end of the purpose, return authorised records and remove remaining copies, subject to lawful retention. Identify required retained copies, restrict use and continue protection; document backup expiry honestly. Follow the [retention policy](../../policies/records-and-data-retention-policy.md).
6. **Rights and duration.** No licence or ownership transfer is implied beyond the permitted use. Set a proportionate duration and post-termination obligation for the actual information and relationship; do not insert a universal perpetual restriction on all knowledge.
7. **Breach handling.** Preserve evidence, mitigate damage and cooperate with investigation. Remedies and any employment consequences are determined under the applicable agreement and law; this template sets no automatic penalty.
