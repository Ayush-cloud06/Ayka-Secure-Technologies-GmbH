> Ayka Secure Technologies GmbH is a simulated company. These are proposed requirements; a written policy is not evidence that its controls operate.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Handle alleged breaches of security duties consistently and fairly (ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.6.4). Applies to staff; contractor matters follow the relevant contract and supplier process. Security containment and employment decisions are distinct: restricting a potentially compromised account does not establish misconduct.

This draft has no disciplinary cases. It does not set legal entitlements or replace case-specific advice. Local employment, privacy and representation requirements must be checked before use.

## 2. Responsibilities and safeguards

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR & Administration | Coordinate confidential fact-finding, notify the affected person, retain records and check procedural safeguards |
| Security Operations | Preserve relevant evidence and investigate technical facts within authorised scope |
| CISO | Assess security impact and immediate containment; identify systemic weaknesses |
| Compliance & Risk Management | Assess privacy, permitted evidence use and legal/representation requirements with appropriate advice |
| Managing Director | Decide proportionate action within lawful authority; arrange independent handling where there is a conflict |

No retaliation for good-faith security reports or protected disclosures. Consider unclear instructions, missing training, system design, workload and accidental error before attributing blame. Do not use this process to suppress reporting. An allegation against a decision-maker requires an uninvolved authorised reviewer; none is available today under the single-maintainer model (RISK-005).

## 3. Legal and representation checks

Before investigation or a decision, check applicable employment/contract rules, employee notice, lawful evidence collection, hearing/representation rights and any works-council involvement. The official [BetrVG §87(1), particularly nos. 1 and 6](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/betrvg/__87.html), read 2026-10-14, addresses co-determination for workplace conduct and certain monitoring systems, subject to its statutory conditions. It is not a complete checklist for an individual disciplinary action.

No works council or actual employment relationship is evidenced in this simulation. HR & Administration must establish the facts and obtain appropriate advice; this policy does not authorise unrestricted surveillance, access to personal devices or automatic dismissal. Other case-specific duties and time limits remain **to verify** in the [legal register](../00-context-and-governance/legal-and-regulatory-register.md).

## 4. Procedure

1. **Receive and contain.** Record allegation, source, date and immediate risk in a restricted case record. Security Operations follows [incident response](../../policies/incident-response-plan.md) where needed and preserves evidence without assuming guilt.
2. **Assign an impartial case lead.** HR & Administration checks conflicts, scope, authorisation and section 3 requirements. Limit evidence to relevant facts and preserve source, access history and integrity.
3. **Establish facts.** Document the duty communicated, relevant training, event sequence, technical evidence and alternative explanations. Distinguish proven facts from allegations and unresolved questions.
4. **Hear the response.** Explain the allegation and give a fair opportunity to respond with representation where applicable. Record the response and correct inaccurate evidence before a decision.
5. **Decide proportionately.** The authorised decision-maker records evidence, mitigating factors and rationale. Possible outcomes include no action, clarification, training, restricted duties, a legally reviewed warning or another lawful contractual/employment measure. No outcome is automatic.
6. **Communicate and allow review.** Provide the decision, required actions and a review route handled by an uninvolved authorised person. Actual deadlines and rights must be established for the case.
7. **Close and improve.** Verify action completion, remove temporary restrictions when safe, set record expiry and refer systemic causes to the [corrective-action procedure](../06-operational-procedures/corrective-action-procedure.md). Publish only anonymised lessons where suitable.

## 5. Record and status

Record case ID, dates, authority, allegation, evidence locations, person response, legal/representation checks, decision/reasons, reviewer, actions, closure and retention expiry. Restrict access to people handling the case; keep the record outside this public repository under the [retention policy](../../policies/records-and-data-retention-policy.md).
