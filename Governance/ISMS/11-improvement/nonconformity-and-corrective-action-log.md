## 1. Purpose and entry criteria

Record evidenced failures against an applicable requirement and their corrective actions under ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause 10.2. Follow the [corrective action procedure](../06-operational-procedures/corrective-action-procedure.md). A risk, proposed improvement or unapproved draft requirement alone does not establish a nonconformity. Existing design gaps stay in the [risk register](../03-risk-management/risk-register.md) until evaluated against an applicable criterion.

## 2. Register schema and state

Use `NC-<year>-<sequence>` for the issue and `CA-<year>-<sequence>` where multiple actions need separate ownership. Each issue requires source/date, exact requirement, objective evidence, consequence/scope, correction, root cause, review of similar exposure, action plan, owner, due date and effectiveness method/date. Preserve owner responses and disputed facts.

Statuses: Reported → Confirmed → Action in progress → Awaiting effectiveness review → Closed. Record Reopened after failed verification; record Not substantiated with rationale if evidence does not establish a failure. An action implemented in source may await runtime verification and must stay open for that requirement. Never substitute a new policy file for evidence that behavior changed.

## 4. Oversight and records

CISO checks open/overdue entries monthly, reports [M-09](../08-monitoring-and-measurement/kpis-and-metrics.md) with its population and presents recurring or material issues at [management review](../10-management-review/management-review-agenda.md). Managing Director resolves overdue material risk or resources. Retain original evidence, decision history and closure verification under the [retention policy](../../policies/records-and-data-retention-policy.md); do not erase a closed or rejected record.
