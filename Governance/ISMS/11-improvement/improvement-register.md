Ayka Secure Technologies GmbH is a simulated company; this document does not establish a performed activity.

## 1. Purpose and relationship to other records

Track evidence-informed opportunities to improve the suitability, adequacy and effectiveness of the ISMS under ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause 10.1. Use [corrective action](../06-operational-procedures/corrective-action-procedure.md) where a requirement has failed; use the [risk treatment plan](../03-risk-management/risk-treatment-plan.md) for existing risks. This register links those records when useful but does not duplicate their authoritative status.

## 2. Proposal and selection

Any function owner may submit a proposal with source/date, problem or opportunity, affected objectives/assets/controls, expected benefit, measurable success criterion, cost/effort, dependencies and potential adverse effects. CISO evaluates urgency, risk reduction, feasibility and evidence quality. Managing Director approves resource or scope changes; record rejected/deferred proposals with a reason and reconsideration trigger.

Implementation follows [change management](../06-operational-procedures/change-management-procedure.md) and [document control](../00-context-and-governance/document-control-procedure.md). Require a baseline and observation method before implementation so that the owner can demonstrate improvement, rather than counting documents or changes as benefit.

## 4. Review and preservation

CISO reviews active proposals quarterly and submits decisions requiring authority to [management review](../10-management-review/management-review-agenda.md). Link consequential [lessons](lessons-learned-log.md), risk reassessments and metrics. Keep rejected proposals and earlier decisions to avoid repeated unsupported suggestions. Apply the [retention policy](../../policies/records-and-data-retention-policy.md).
