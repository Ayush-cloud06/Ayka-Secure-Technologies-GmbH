Ayka Secure Technologies GmbH is a simulated company; this document does not establish a performed activity.

## 1. Purpose and entry rules

Capture lessons from actual incidents, exercises, audits, recovery tests, changes and corrective actions under ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.27 and clause 10.1. An event must have a dated source and an observed result. Hypothetical scenarios belong in plans; a template cannot establish that a lesson was learned or shared.

## 2. Review method

Security Operations coordinates a review after a significant incident/exercise or failed recovery/change, using the timing set by the [incident plan](../../policies/incident-response-plan.md) or relevant [change procedure](../06-operational-procedures/change-management-procedure.md). Include actual participants from the affected functions and separate facts, inferred causes and unresolved questions.

Document expected behavior, actual sequence, what helped, what failed, contributing conditions and effects on other systems or teams. Discuss process and technical causes rather than assigning unsupported blame. Record evidence that challenges the initial explanation. Identify a concrete change or an explicit no-change decision with rationale; lessons without follow-up do not demonstrate improvement.

## 4. Follow-up and retention

Create [corrective actions](nonconformity-and-corrective-action-log.md) for evidenced failed requirements and [improvement proposals](improvement-register.md) for other opportunities. Update risk/treatment, procedures or training when approved; record the linked revision and actual communication. CISO reviews unresolved lessons at [management review](../10-management-review/management-review-agenda.md). Preserve source evidence and earlier interpretations under the [retention policy](../../policies/records-and-data-retention-policy.md).
