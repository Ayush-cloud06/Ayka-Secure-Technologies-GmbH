## 1. Purpose and entry criteria

Track findings from an actually performed [internal audit](audit-programme.md), with a requirement and objective evidence. This file is an empty controlled register, not a report or completed assessment. Design gaps already recorded in the [risk register](../03-risk-management/risk-register.md) remain there until an auditor actually examines them; copying them here would falsely imply an audit occurred.

## 2. Finding classification and workflow

| Class | Meaning and handling |
|---|---|
| Nonconformity | Evidence that an applicable requirement was not fulfilled; identify requirement, observation and scope; assess significance and route through [corrective action](../06-operational-procedures/corrective-action-procedure.md) |
| Observation | Evidence-supported concern without a demonstrated failed requirement; state uncertainty and further evidence needed |
| Improvement opportunity | Proposed benefit beyond demonstrated conformity; link to [improvement register](../11-improvement/improvement-register.md) |

Auditor validates facts with the process owner, retains disagreements, and issues the finding. CISO assigns an action owner and risk-based target date; Managing Director resolves conflicts and overdue material issues. Only the auditor or another independent competent reviewer verifies closure against the original requirement. A process owner cannot close a finding solely by stating that the fix is complete.

Statuses: Open → Response agreed → Action in progress → Awaiting effectiveness review → Closed; reopen if the corrective action is ineffective. Record cancellation/duplicate resolution with a reason and retained link rather than deleting history. These statuses track work, not control implementation maturity.

## 4. Review, reporting and retention

Compliance & Risk Management checks open findings monthly once entries exist and before [management review](../10-management-review/management-review-agenda.md). Report unresolved high-risk findings, overdue actions, recurrence and evidence limitations. No entries does not mean no nonconformities: here it means no audit. Follow the [retention policy](../../policies/records-and-data-retention-policy.md). ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clauses 9.2 and 10.2.
