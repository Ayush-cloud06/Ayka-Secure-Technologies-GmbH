Ayka Secure Technologies GmbH is a simulated company. This is a proposed selection of controls, not certification or a record of management approval. Assessment snapshot: 2026-10-14.

## 1. Basis and decision rules

This register addresses clause 6.1.3(d) of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 against the [ISMS scope](../00-context-and-governance/scope.md), [risk register](../03-risk-management/risk-register.md), [risk treatment plan](../03-risk-management/risk-treatment-plan.md), and [legal register](../00-context-and-governance/legal-and-regulatory-register.md). The control IDs cover all 93 Annex A:2022 entries; the short working labels below describe their subject and do not reproduce normative control text. The [ISO catalogue](https://www.iso.org/standard/82875.html) identifies the management-system standard; use a licensed copy for normative assessment.

**Applicability:** Yes means selected for the proposed scope, including supplier oversight. All 93 are selected; none are excluded. Absent implementation is a gap, not a reason for exclusion. Provider facilities, remote work and prospective outsourced development retain relevant responsibilities even when Ayka does not operate the facility or currently use an external developer. Review this conservative selection when actual contracts, premises and processing are known; no exclusion is approved by this draft.

**Status:** Implemented requires operating evidence for the stated scope; Partial means a named subset operates with remaining gaps; Gated design means scanned workload Terraform, never deployed; Designed means documentation or ungated platform Terraform; Planned means the control-specific design is still missing. Planned rows may link related requirements that do not yet design that control. No complete Annex A control is labelled Implemented. A Partial CI process does not prove the proposed company operates that process.

Only `Internal-IT/workloads/ayka-portal` passes through the [compliance workflow](../../../.github/workflows/terraform-workflow.yml). Its refresh-free plan and simulated apply are not runtime evidence. Platform and identity roots remain Designed. A mapping ID denotes a repository cross-reference, not satisfaction of the whole Annex A control. The [gap analysis](control-gap-analysis.md) explains these limits, including RISK-011: target `eu-central-1` differs from `ap-south-1` in existing roots.

## 2. Control selection and evidence

Every row gives its inclusion rationale, current status, evidence or related design, a responsible role and remaining work. Gap IDs refer to the [treatment workstreams](control-gap-analysis.md). All rows have `Yes` applicability; exclusion justification is therefore not applicable. Before approval, the CISO must confirm selections and statuses, and the Managing Director must approve the SoA and treatment resources through the [document control procedure](../00-context-and-governance/document-control-procedure.md).
