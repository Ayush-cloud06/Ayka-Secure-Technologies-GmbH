## 4. Your rights

Access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), portability (Art. 20), and withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3)). Write to the contact above. We answer within one month (Art. 12(3)). You can complain to the supervisory authority: the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg, or the authority of your place of residence.

## 5. Right to object (Art. 21)

**You can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f), which covers the server logs and enquiry handling above.** We then stop unless we show compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing is needed for legal claims (Art. 21(1)). Where we ever use your data for direct marketing, you can object at any time without giving reasons and we stop (Art. 21(2), (3)). This paragraph is shown separately from the rest, as Art. 21(4) requires.
