## 1. Purpose and scope

Reduce exposure of information left on desks, printers, meeting spaces and screens (ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.7.7). Applies to staff and contractors at the proposed Stuttgart office, home workspaces, customer sites and public locations. Requirements supplement [classification](data-classification-and-confidentiality-policy.md), [remote work](remote-work-policy.md) and [endpoint security](user-endpoint-device-policy.md).

## 2. Required handling

| Situation | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Leaving a device, even briefly | Lock the screen immediately. Automatic lock is a backstop of at most 5 minutes, with authentication required on wake |
| Leaving a desk or finishing work | Put non-public papers and removable media in locked storage; secure the device; remove exposed credentials and access tokens |
| Meeting rooms and shared displays | Check participants and screen-sharing scope; close unrelated applications and notifications; clear whiteboards and retrieve papers after the meeting |
| Printing | Avoid printing Confidential information; use attended or secure-release printing if approved; retrieve every page, including rejected pages |
| Waste | Put non-public papers in secure destruction storage; use an assessed disposal service or appropriate shredder; never place them in ordinary recycling |
| Visitors and cleaners | Prevent sight of non-public records; escort visitors under local access arrangements; remove sensitive material before access |
| Home and public locations | Keep work information from household members; use privacy filters when needed; no unattended sensitive paperwork |
| End of employment or contract | Return access cards, papers, media and devices under [offboarding](../ISMS/01-organization/onboarding-and-offboarding-procedure.md); verify remaining copies |

## 3. Reporting and review

Report exposed credentials, missing papers or unintended screen disclosure immediately under [incident response](incident-response-plan.md). Contain the exposure without deleting incident evidence. HR & Administration arranges a workplace check quarterly once premises and staff exist; Cloud Platform Engineering verifies device lock settings. Findings record location category, date, issue and action without photographs of sensitive content.

CISO exceptions require a reason, equivalent protection and an expiry of at most 90 days. No exception allows leaving credentials exposed. Repeated or deliberate noncompliance follows the [disciplinary process](../ISMS/01-organization/disciplinary-process.md).
