## What the page said

Read through a fetch tool that returns the text in fragments, not a saved copy of the page:

- § 38(1) sentence 1: in addition to Art. 37(1)(b) and (c), controller and processor designate a data protection officer "soweit sie in der Regel mindestens 20 Personen ständig mit der automatisierten Verarbeitung personenbezogener Daten beschäftigen".
- § 38(1) sentence 2: a DPO must be designated regardless of the number of persons where the processing is subject to a data protection impact assessment under Art. 35, or where personal data is processed commercially for transfer, anonymised transfer, or market or opinion research.
- § 38(2): § 6(4), (5) sentence 2 and (6) apply; § 6(4) only where designation is mandatory.
